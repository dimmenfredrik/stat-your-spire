OPTIONAL: your own pictures
===========================
Put image files here (png, webp, jpg, gif or svg) and STS2 Coach shows them
next to the matching card or enemy name. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Name each file after the card or enemy in lowercase, with underscores for
spaces and symbols:
   Foolish Shot  ->  foolish_shot.png
   Phrog Parasite -> phrog_parasite.png

Do not upload copyrighted game art to a public place (GitHub etc).
