PICTURES
========
The first time you start STS2 Coach it builds the card, relic, potion and event
pictures plus short descriptions from YOUR OWN installed Slay the Spire 2
(about 30 seconds, one time). Nothing is downloaded or uploaded: the files are
made on your PC from your own game and stay in this folder.

If the game is not found, the coach still works, just without pictures.
To rebuild after a game update: delete descriptions.json in this folder and
start the coach again.

You can also add your own images here (png, webp, jpg, gif or svg), named in
lowercase with underscores, e.g. foolish_shot.png. Do not upload game art to a
public place (GitHub etc).
